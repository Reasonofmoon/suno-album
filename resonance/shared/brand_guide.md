# RESONANCE Brand Guide

> "Create once. Stream forever. Earn while they focus."

## Voice

- Calm, practical, focus-first.
- No hype language.
- Promise: long-form background utility.

## Naming Convention

- Album ID: `T##_V##`
- Title pattern:
  - `Theme Vol.N - Benefit Hook | Duration`
  - Example: `Cafe Jazz Vol.3 - Cozy Coffee Shop Focus | 3 Hours`

## Typography

- **Title**: Bold, clean sans-serif (Poppins / Pretendard)
- **Subtitle**: Light weight, same family
- **Duration Badge**: Monospace or semi-bold

## Thumbnail Rules

- Keep type large and readable at phone size.
- Show duration on the card (long = trust signal).
- Keep one consistent frame system across series.
- Avoid clutter; 1 visual center + 1 text block.
- Vol. number for series continuity.

## Export Specs

- Thumbnail: `1280x720` minimum, `1920x1080` preferred.
- File size: `< 2MB`.
- Video: `1920x1080`, `30fps`, `H.264`.
